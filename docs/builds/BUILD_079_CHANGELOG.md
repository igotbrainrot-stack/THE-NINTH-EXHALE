# The Ninth Exhale — Build 07.9, focused playable gameplay pass

## Release scope
This **does not complete every redesign request** in the Build 07.9 directive. It is an implemented, additive gameplay-focused first pass over the real Build 07.8 project. No independent executable Godot runtime was available to verify compilation or live playability in this authoring environment.

## Stage A — combat, opening, three differentiated encounters (implemented in source)

- Corrected enemy fallback placement: an actor without a saved position retains its authored placement rather than materializing at the old generic guard coordinates.
- Enemy perception now checks an actual player sightline before initiating most ranged attacks, and remembers the last confirmed location for four seconds instead of tracking through walls forever.
- Nearby hostiles defer overlapping wind-ups, creating more readable, staggered commitments.
- Bullet strikes create short, material-sensitive 3D shards for metal, ceramic, stone or living targets; no new ammunition or loot is generated by effects.
- Added restrained reticle hit confirmation and momentary incoming damage tint.
- Added **three different spatial encounter dangers** to existing areas, each telegraphing a marked floor and remaining avoidable:
  1. **Hearth Nine** — a small electrical-service arc during the tutorial crawler alarm (4 HP danger, 2.15m radius, finite safety isolator).
  2. **Sootworks** — a broader furnace pressure cycle during active Millwright enforcement (7 HP danger, 3.25m radius, optional manual brake or destructive rupture).
  3. **Flood Basilica** — timed acoustic pressure that reduces **Breath**, not HP, only during hostile signal enforcement (7 Breath per contact, 4.05m radius, safe-window tuning).
- Safety levers work directly in the 3D world with E. They are also shootable. Quiet isolation costs 1 Energy + 1 Labour; destructive rupture costs 2 Energy. These are finite, saved actions with a ledger entry, no free rewards.
- The existing zero-ammunition F intervention, nonviolent district routes and one-time Writ recoveries remain intact.

## Stage B — quest/world reactivity (initial pass)

- Helping Brin removes the active coercive foundry pressure cycle on subsequent inspection of that location.
- Helping Ossa makes the coercive Basilica acoustic danger go quiet.
- Quietly tuning the Basilica signal adds one *factual observation* to the existing Rootkin hearing, not an owned creature or an automatic win.
- Physical safety levers visibly remain isolated after save/load. The key gameplay opportunity is to navigate an encounter and choose a finite repair or a destructive approach instead of only clicking a modal.

## Stage C — more physical tactical and civic feedback (initial pass)

- The existing five-command public hearing displays a real-world field circle, coloured phase, current integrity and strain. Values are derived from the authoritative state; the existing hearing commands and fair retries remain unchanged.
- The public assembly has six physically visible constituency statuses. They only show ATTESTED when the game's *existing* evidence-backed support function approves an actual recorded vote.
- Physical rail tokens represent **staged cohorts**, never boarded passengers. Mandate lights distinguish present/revoked/unresolved states without inventing voices.

## Stage D — bounded variation (initial pass)

- The environmental cycle offset is reproducibly derived from the WorldPlan seed modulo three. The existing seeded cover and optional enemy selection systems are preserved.
- No random room graph or mandatory-progression randomization has been introduced in this release.

## Stage E — art/audio (initial pass)

- Original pressure-docket floor graphic, generated reproducibly by `art/tools/generate_pressure_decal_079.py`.
- Three original synthesized WAV danger effects, generated reproducibly by `art/tools/generate_gameplay_sfx_079.py`.
- Two richer, externally hosted original Runway sound effects were also commissioned; their actual audio bytes are **not embedded**. The playable source uses the newly created local WAV effects.
